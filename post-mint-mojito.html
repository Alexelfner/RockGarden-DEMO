Rock Garden Mint Mojito Recipe

What’s in a Mojito?

  • Rock Garden Fresh mint
  • Simple syrup: one part sugar, one part water, simmer until dissolved
  • Lime wedges: Fresh squeezed is preferred over bottled lime juice!
  • White rum: Clear white rum is best! Make sure to choose a good quality rum.
  • Sparkling water: Is added at the end to top off our glass.
  • Ice

Preparation

  1. Cut up your lime, place 4 lime wedges in a glass.
  2. Toss about 10 leaves of fresh Rock Garden Mint.
  3. Muddle.
  4. Add a double shot of rum.
  5. Add a single shot of simple syrup.
  6. Add ice and top it off with sparkling water.
