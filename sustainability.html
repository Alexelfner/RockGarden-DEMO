Sustainability

Rock Garden, We Grow and we're green.

We’ve been active in practicing what is preached. Besides utilizing the philosophy of Reduce, Reuse and Recycle in our growing and packing facilities, we can add Reclaim.

A few years ago we took steps to reclaim abandoned railroad land behind our packing house. The land was cleared of debris and now is home to the production of specialty herbs and microgreens. An oasis in an urban environment!
