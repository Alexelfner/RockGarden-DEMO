We Grow Herbs

Everyone claims they have fresh herbs but not everyone can meet the growing demands of today’s fresh food market. Since our first crop in 1977, Rock Garden has grown – literally. Our Agronomists manage an extensive farming network of both conventional and organic herbs that span two hemispheres and more than 5 countries, including the United States. This strategically located farming infrastructure has allowed us to maintain a continual supply of fresh herbs, baby greens and specialty produce to the market year round without disruption.

We grow Organic and Conventional:

  • Basil
  • Chives
  • Cilantro
  • Culantro
  • Dill
  • Mint
  • Parsley
  • Rosemary
  • Sage
  • Tarragon
  • Thyme
