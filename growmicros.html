We Grow Microgreens

Packed with powerful nutrients and a burst of flavor

Packed with powerful nutrients and a burst of flavor, Rock Garden's organic microgreens transform ordinary dishes into tasty, healthy meals.

Research has found some microgreens contain up to 40 times higher levels of vital nutrients than their mature counterparts. Top chefs from across the globe originally used microgreens as an adornment for their creative dishes. Only recently, Rock Garden has made microgreens more accessible to the consumer by creating a variety of flavor profiles designed to compliment a host of dishes.

New Microgreen Blends

Fiesta-Mix

Bring the party to any dish.

Fiesta Mix is a wonderful mixture of assorted organic edible flowers and organic microgreens. It can be a fun and tasty addition to any dish for any occasion.

Tex-Mex

The healthy way to dress up a taco.

Tex-Mex is a modern combination of Onions, Cilantro, Parsley, and Radish with a flavor reminiscent of your favorite Mexican dishes. Try me as a Taco or Enchilada topping or on your favorite soup!

Veggies Galore

For the best dressed sandwich.

Dress up your favorite sandwich or add a burst of tasty flavor to any salad. Veggies Galore is an earthy blend of Radish, Collards and Amaranth with the unmistakable taste of fresh cut vegetables.

Kale Combo

Have an even healthier salad.

Kale Combo is a buttery blend of Kale, Kohlrabi and Broccoli, recognized as some of the earth’s healthiest greens. Try me as a flavor filled compliment to any salad dish!

Italian Blend

Turn pizza into a gourmet slice.

A great new pizza topper! Italian Blend is a modern combination of Arugula, Basil and Parsley with flavor reminiscent of favorite Italian dishes. Try me as a pizza topping!

Spicy Selection

Add a kick to burgers.

A zesty new burger topper! An intense fusion of Radish, Daikon and Cress that will awaken the taste buds with hints of pepper. Try me on burgers and more!
