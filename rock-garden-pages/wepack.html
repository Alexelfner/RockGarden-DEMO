We Pack

Retail packages – all shapes and sizes.

Everyone claims they have fresh herbs and microgreens but not everyone can meet the growing demands of today’s fresh food market. Chances are, the herbs you're already purchasing were grown and packed by us.

We can pack under the Rock Garden brand or pack under your label for your retail needs.

The ability to offer not only the best quality product possible, but also to create breakthrough packaging, sets Rock Garden apart from other growers. We’ve been involved in the development of bags and clam shells that extend the shelf life of packaged produce that are now a standard throughout the industry.

Rock Garden continues to push the envelope and utilize new technologies in creation of the next generation of fresh produce packaging. Rock Garden, we pack conventional and organic herbs and microgreens in various size clamshells and bags, Hang them or stand them up. Inquire about custom packaging for your retail needs.
