We Ship Fresh and Fast.

From planting to harvesting, and every step along the way, we take great care and tremendous pride to insure the products that reach your shelves, kitchens and dinner plates, are fresh, tasty and safe. It's as important to us as it is to you and your customer.

We pack and ship herbs and microgreens year round by truck, domestic air carrier, or express mail carrier; making sure they not only arrive fresh, but that they arrive when you need them.
